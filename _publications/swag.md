## Abstract

SWAG extends **3D Gaussian Splatting** to unconstrained image collections by introducing appearance-conditioned Gaussians and transient object modeling. The approach reconstructs photorealistic scenes from Internet-scale imagery while remaining compatible with real-time rendering.

## Key Contributions

- Appearance-conditioned Gaussian Splatting
- Transient object modeling
- Robust reconstruction from in-the-wild images
- Real-time novel-view synthesis
- State-of-the-art performance on outdoor landmarks
